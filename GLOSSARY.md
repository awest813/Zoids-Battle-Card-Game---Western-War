## Style rules

1. **Keep UI labels short.** The original boxes are sized for 4–8 fullwidth characters. Prefer one-word verbs and abbreviations.
2. **Use sentence case for dialogue**, title case for menu labels.
3. **Preserve the speaker's tone.** The battle voice lines are intentionally rough or pompous; match the character, not literal Japanese.
4. **Do not translate card stats as prose.** Keep numbers and symbols unchanged.
5. **Keep `%s`/`%d` placeholders intact** if the runtime table uses them.
6. **Use "Zoid" as a mass noun in UI** ("Zoid Deck", "Zoid Card"), and pluralize normally in dialogue ("Zoids").
7. **Romanize the subtitle consistently:** `Zoids: Battle Card Game — Chronicle of the Western Continent` is the literal title; `Zoids: Battle Card Game — Western Continental War` is the working UI title. Pick one and use it everywhere.
